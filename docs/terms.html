What RepToll is

RepToll shields apps you choose and lets you earn time in them with exercise counted by the camera. It is a self-control tool, not a fitness, medical or physiotherapy service.

Exercise safely

Only do exercises you can do safely. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, and ask a doctor before starting if you have a health condition, an injury or are pregnant. You can always use your daily free minutes or change your toll to a gentler exercise or level. Rep counting is an estimate and can miss or add reps.

Shields

Shields rely on Apple's Screen Time. iOS decides when the shield appears and may delay or skip it in some situations. Phone calls and Emergency SOS always work; any other app is shielded only if you pick it. You can remove RepToll's Screen Time access at any time in Settings.

Subscriptions

RepToll Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. A free trial, where offered, converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends; any unused portion of a trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
