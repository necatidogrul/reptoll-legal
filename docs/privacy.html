Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

RepToll has no account and no server. The apps you choose to shield, your minutes, reps and streak are stored only on your device. The camera counts your reps on the device and no image ever leaves it.

Screen Time (Family Controls)

RepToll uses Apple's Screen Time framework to shield the apps and websites you pick and to count the minutes you spend in them. Apple gives RepToll only opaque tokens for your selection: RepToll cannot see the names of the apps or websites you picked, what you do inside them, or your browsing history. Usage minutes are measured by iOS and passed to RepToll's on-device extension only as "this many minutes used", so the shield can come back when your earned time runs out. None of this is sent anywhere.

Camera

When you pay a toll, RepToll uses the front camera and Apple's on-device Vision framework to find body joints and count push-ups, squats or hold time. Frames are analysed in memory and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded, and the camera is only on while the counting screen is open.

Notifications

RepToll schedules local notifications on your device (for example "tap to pay the toll" from the shield, and a reminder before a free trial ends). No push service or server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. RepToll checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on the device. We never see your payment details, name or email.

Analytics and advertising

RepToll contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your rights

Everything RepToll stores is on your device, so you control it completely. Deleting the app deletes it all, and removing RepToll's Screen Time access in Settings lifts every shield. There is nothing on our side to request, correct or erase; if you would like written confirmation of that, email us.

Children

RepToll is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. RepToll uses Screen Time for the person using the device ("individual" authorisation), not parental supervision of another person.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
