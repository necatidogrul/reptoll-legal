The shield did not come back

Earned minutes are counted only while you are in the apps you picked, so the shield returns when they are used up, not after a fixed time. If it still does not return, open RepToll once: it re-checks the shield on launch. Check Settings → Screen Time is on and RepToll is allowed.

My reps are not counted

Put the phone on the floor or against a wall so your whole body is in view, in good light. For push-ups, film from the side. The counter only counts a rep that goes deep enough and comes back up.

I need an app right now

Use your free minutes on the home screen, or change the toll. Phone calls and Emergency SOS always work, and only the apps you picked are shielded.

How do I turn RepToll off?

Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → RepToll, or simply delete the app. Every shield is lifted.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the Pro screen, tap Restore with the same Apple ID you bought with.
